# Timetable — Student guide

The *Timetable* block shows your calendar events: due dates of activities, course and group events and your
own events. On your Dashboard it shows the events of all your courses; in a course, the events of that course.

## Switching views

The buttons at the top of the block switch between the views your teacher or administrator offers:

- **Today** — today's events.
- **This week** — the days of this week. Select a day to see its events.
- **Upcoming** — your events from today on.

When there are more events than the block shows at a time, use **Previous events** and **Next events** below
them. Events that have ended are dimmed.

Select an event to see its details, such as the time and the activity it belongs to. **Go to calendar** at the
bottom of the block opens the full calendar.

## If an event is missing

- **Upcoming** shows as many days as your *Upcoming events look-ahead*. Change it under *your user menu ▸
  Preferences ▸ Calendar preferences*.
- The week starts on the *First day of week* of the same preferences.
- In a course, the block shows only that course's events. Site events are on your Dashboard.
- Group events are shown to the members of the group.
- Events of activities that are hidden from you are not shown.
